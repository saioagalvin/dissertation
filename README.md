# dissertation
My MMath (Hons) Mathematics dissertation, analysing variables that affect wildland recovery from wildfire. Case study: Kenow Fire (2017), Southern Alberta, Canada.

## Description
Case study of the Kenow Fire in 2017, in the Waterton Lakes National Park, Alberta, Canada. Focusing on analysing Sentinel-2 satellite imaging and performing statistical analysis.

## Key papers
-  Post-fire vegetation regeneration during abnormally dry years following severe montane fire: Southern Alberta, Canada by J. Aspinall and L. Chasmer and C.A. Coburn and C. Hopkinson, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foreco.2025.122750
-  Evaluating forest fire severity and vegetative recovery using Sentinel imagery and spectral indices in a cloud-based environment by Sercan Gülci and Michael G. Wing, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asr.2025.07.044

## Data sources
- ESA Sentinel-2, NIR and SWIR bands
- ESA CCI land cover data, yearly (2012 - 2022)
- Field data
